Dinosaur sound previews

Twelve audition drafts made from free recorded animal calls and artist-made effects. These are imagined dinosaur voices, not authentic extinct-animal recordings. Nothing in this preview has been added to the app.

T-Rex

Cinematic roar with a low animal growl underneath.

Triceratops

Two throaty grunts followed by a rounded growl.

Stegosaurus

Short low grunts with a breathy finish.

Brachiosaurus

A long resonant animal call with a low undertone.

Diplodocus

Two low, rounded calls with a pause between them.

Ankylosaurus

A snorting grunt and a short low rumble.

Brontosaurus

A deep sustained bellow, shaped from an animal call.

Spinosaurus

A raspier roar mixed with an alligator growl.

Pteranodon

Three short squawking calls.

Quetzalcoatlus

Lower, longer croaking calls for the larger pterosaur.

Parasaurolophus

A resonant horn-like call inspired by its crest.

Velociraptor

A quick sequence of sharper calls and chirrs.
