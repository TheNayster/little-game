# Zoo animal model sheet drafts

September 30, 2026. Four generated visual development sheets for the Little Weeps zoo requested by the user. The user confirmed that animals should fit the Bluey show's 2D art direction.

- `savanna-model-sheet.png`: African elephant, giraffe, plains zebra and lion.
- `living-dinosaurs-model-sheet.png`: Tyrannosaurus rex, Triceratops, Brachiosaurus and Stegosaurus.
- `aquarium-model-sheet.png`: clownfish, regal blue tang, adult zebra shark and African penguin.
- `reptile-model-sheet.png`: green iguana, leopard gecko, Galapagos tortoise and Nile crocodile.

Each sheet provides front, side, back and feeding views, with a small palette. The side view guides gameplay. Read the [research and movement contract](../../../docs/implementation/zoo-world-research-2026-09-30.md) for routines, appropriate food, four-player feeding and sound plans.

These are drafts generated with the built-in imagegen tool, not approved final sprites, animation frames, editable layered rigs or scientific reconstructions. `generation-prompts.json` preserves the exact prompts, tool and checksums. The images were visually inspected for category labels, subject coverage, turnaround columns, food poses and overall 2D readability.

Before production, trace into editable layered sources and simplify shading to match the game's flat Bluey direction. Resolve individual spots/stripes between poses, proportion and fin/toe details, Brachiosaurus forelimb length and stegosaur plate rows. Cell sizing is for readability and is not a shared animal height scale. Do not slice the backgrounds into a sprite atlas and call the animals animated. Separate trunk, jaw, eyes, near/far limbs, fins/flippers and tails, and record interaction sockets and pivots.

Dinosaurs are live in the proposed game world. Their color and calls are authored choices. Prepared meat/fish portions avoid a hunting depiction while preserving broad food categories. No game asset import, audio download, build, save change or device installation happened in this research task.
