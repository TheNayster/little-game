# Activity review template

Use this section once per game or direct activity in its world audit. Write in plain language for the parent and for a ChatGPT Classic handoff. Do not call an activity reviewed until its current flow and pictures have evidence.

- Status: not reviewed, in progress, reviewed with limits, or needs refresh:

## Story and player goal

Explain who needs help, what starts the play and why the child would care. If the activity is open play, say that rather than inventing a story or score.

## Play and completion

Write the real numbered steps from entry to finish, including meaningful choices, wrong attempts, helpful feedback and replay. State the exact end condition, or the outcome of an open-play interaction. Explain what each sibling contributes and what happens if one leaves. Separate optional decoration from required progress.

## Buttons and placement

| Visible label or picture | Screen position or scene object | What the input actually does | When it appears or becomes enabled | Problem and screenshot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awaiting inspection | Awaiting inspection | Awaiting inspection | Awaiting inspection | No finding yet |

Record direct taps, drag, hold and movement gestures as well as menu buttons. Check consistent exit/help positions, scene occlusion, small or unclear targets, hidden scrolling, conflicting controls and disabled actions taking space. Do not infer touch target suitability from nominal code dimensions alone.

## NPC purpose and behavior

Identify each role, how the player recognizes it, its position, props, movement, dialogue, practical help and response to progress. Distinguish an authored job from decorative movement. Record saved cast behavior and four-player crowding where relevant.

## Screenshots and observations

Link real entry, active play, choice or mistake, and completion or open-play outcome screenshots with captions. Record build, viewport, player count and observed behavior. Include a phone view and one relevant tablet or four-player composition view, reusing matching existing evidence where appropriate. Never label PC aspect captures as physical-device screenshots.

## Research and recommendations

Link the relevant existing project notes. If focused new research is needed, use primary developer, publisher, official design or technical sources, record the access date and explain exactly what they support. Keep proposed adaptations distinct from existing play and do not claim a comparison game was played without doing so.

## Findings and priorities

| Finding ID | Observed issue and consequence | Evidence | Proposed improvement | Priority and effort | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | No current finding recorded | Pending | Pending | Pending | Not reviewed |

Use priority 1 for an observed blocker to understandable or usable play, priority 2 for presentation or interaction improvement, and priority 3 for optional expansion. Tie each recommendation to an observed problem; additions remain proposals.

## Handoff and remaining limits

Provide a copyable story/mechanics/UI summary, the smallest useful next improvement and unresolved questions. Record what was actually reviewed, any stale evidence and parent feedback still needed. A compiled game or completed checklist is not proof of fun.
