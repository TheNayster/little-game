## Authority, ownership and safe edits

Committed `ElephantProp` records carry stable monotonic `habitat-N` IDs, kind, authored slot, persistent player/profile creator and per-prop revision. Existing Zoo commands, request receipts and shared-world revision checks serialize accepted commits. A placement additionally checks its creation high-water ID; edits check exact prop revision. Same-slot/overlapping claims reject atomically. Rejected moves preserve the original. Failed UI commits discard the stale preview and refresh the expected prop revision/high-water from the received authority state.

Creator-only moving/removal applies in shared play. Disconnect, travel and reconnect retain all placements. No server preview/reservation means disconnect cannot strand a slot lock. No global habitat-clearing button or new destructive parent permission is introduced. **Unavailable creator recovery:** reconnect/select the original enrolled profile through the existing family/profile management. Another child's connection does not inherit edit rights. If that profile's enrollment cannot be recovered, preserve its decorations and use the existing parent recovery workflow; this pilot does not silently transfer ownership or add an administrator bypass.

`usingId` locks the selected prop throughout the authority's approach/use/exit segments. The prop displays a temporary foreground clock picture, drawn as geometry. Move/remove rejects until the action ends and feet exit to the clear front row atY250, or food safely preempts it. Local previews can still be canceled; the committed support remains rendered. Destination type/slot/overlap and revision are rechecked at commit.

## Scheduler and presentation

The existing `AdvanceZoo` scheduler and `ZooLayout` segments own position, phase, age and sequence. `PropWalk` approaches the actual placement's fitted body/trunk anchor; `PropUse` lasts2seconds for gentle rope rubbing/leaf investigation,2.8seconds for quiet canopy rest. `PropExit` retains support until a short vertical walk reachesY250; ordinary wandering then uses that clear feet row while props are present. Rope rubbing uses a small anchored body/ear motion, with the post drawn in front at contact; leafy investigation articulates the original trunk at its fixed leaf holder; canopy rest uses a small settling trunk/ear movement. No competing movement controller, physics or independent client random routines.

Food remains first priority and interrupts approach/use on the next authority tick: **zero scripted additional waiting**, at most0.1seconds authority tick plus transport/snapshot observation. Existing eating completion, water/care order and story clue/acknowledgment retain their scheduling. Placement does not immediately retask a busy animal. A single coalesced pending ID replaces repeated notifications. Quiet idle use waits35–55seconds between eligibility and chooses from committed available props using the existing authority random stream; snapshots do not restart it. No new audio, packages or downloaded assets.

Presentation retains six bounded prop drawings (two display positions × three kinds), fixed in-use badges and three slot targets. Preview geometry is destroyed on replacement/cancel/closure; the modal and retained shapes are destroyed with Zoo. There are no new event subscriptions or per-frame network commands.
