## Step one establish the review baseline

Read AGENTS, current decisions and this world's audit. Record the source commit, reviewed build, installed-device version if actually inspected, viewport and evidence date. Reuse an existing verified artifact and matching current captures where suitable. Keep unfinished changes separate. If no matching playable build is available, record the gap and proceed with clearly labeled source review; do not call stale pictures current.

## Step two explain the actual play

Inventory every current menu game, direct interaction and internal area, keeping planned wishlist items separate. Use the [activity template](world-audits/activity-review-template.md) for each. Explain story, goal, real inputs, choices, mistakes, exact completion or open-play outcome, help, replay, shared contribution and independent departure. Distinguish required steps from decorations and proposed additions.

## Step three capture and review the screen

Use an isolated saved world or consented existing family play; preserve live saves and enrollment. Capture the menu, active interactions, meaningful choices or mistakes and endings. Use a phone composition and a relevant tablet/four-player composition. One focused pass is the default; use existing matching evidence for unchanged play and repeat only for a concrete unresolved issue.

Map visible button labels/pictures and their positions, including direct taps, drag, hold and movement gestures. Review hidden scrolling, obstructed objects, oversized panels, target visibility, disabled actions, help and exit consistency. Record NPC roles, positions, movement, props, dialogue, player attention and progress reactions. Look for four-player overlap without inventing findings from another world.

Screenshots must be real and captioned with build, viewport, player count and observation. PC phone/tablet aspect captures are not physical-device evidence. Keep raw PNGs unchanged; label any future mockups separately. Do not commit private media, saves, credentials, signing material, builds or enrollment logs.

## Step four research a specific problem

Read existing project research first. If a finding needs new comparison or guidance, browse focused primary developer/publisher pages, official design guidance or relevant technical documentation. Record the exact source, access date, what it supports and how the proposed adaptation addresses this game's observed issue. Distinguish screenshots/descriptions from hands-on play, and educational intent from measured child usability. Avoid an unrelated research sweep.

## Step five rank concrete improvements

Give each finding an ID, evidence link, consequence, proposed change, priority and effort. Separate quick framing/control/NPC fixes from new gameplay or story expansions. Name the smallest useful next improvement and whether it changes client presentation, shared rules or saves. Preserve recognized art, established player ownership, saved progress, four-player shared games, varied saved Daycare casts, late joining and independent exits. Suggestions do not imply parent acceptance or completed implementation.

## Step six deliver the review

Update the world's audit with a plain-language summary and relative picture links. Put approved-to-share review files and screenshots in a self-contained folder and ZIP when the parent requests a package. Include a ChatGPT Classic brief, current versus historical evidence labels and remaining limits. Verify file/link presence and ZIP contents; do not include private runtime data.

Documentation delivery follows the standing Git policy: integrate checked documents into main, push, verify the remote commit and remove a finished temporary branch while preserving other unfinished work. Audit completion does not require unrelated game suites or automatic device/server rollout.

## Exit criteria for one world

- Every current activity has a clear entry point, story/goal, actual steps and completion or open-play outcome.
- Controls and gestures are explained with real pictures and accurate placements.
- NPC/animal purpose, camera framing and representative four-player spacing are reviewed.
- Findings cite evidence; research claims and proposed improvements are distinct.
- Evidence has a current verified baseline, or explicitly retained limitations and refresh requirements.
- A prioritized next task and copyable parent/ChatGPT summary are ready.

The phase is complete only when all seven current world reviews meet these criteria. The seven starter files do not mean the reviews are complete. Family judgment about fun remains parent playtesting; compilation and screenshots alone cannot establish it.
