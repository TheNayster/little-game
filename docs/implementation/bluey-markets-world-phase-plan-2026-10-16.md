## Completion contract

- One authoritative shared Markets world, with common station/object state and progress. Where an activity has rounds, use one invitation/start and common progression. Open-ended activities remain open-ended; do not invent a mandatory world timer or shopping quest.
- All four players can contribute together to the same activity. Two ponies or a limited station capacity must not exclude the other children: use visible cooperative roles or fair turns with a common result. NPC assistance may fill missing roles without taking over a human's action.
- Leaving, changing area, disconnecting or switching character releases only that participant's claims and preserves siblings' progress. Preserve player identification independently of the character drawing.
- Character actions are part of the feature: approach, facing, hand/object or animal contact, tool motion, reactions, audio, finish, replay and interrupted return to idle. A working button with a static actor is insufficient.
- Keep PC/VPS authority, private offline saves and authoritative reconnect separation. Never import private offline progress into the shared family. Preserve existing world/save identifiers and compatibility admission.
